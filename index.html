❓ Detective Handbook & Regulations

Instructions, tactical tips, and festival lore for your Ayodhya mystery adventure.

🪔 1. The Core Objective

The holy Eternal Diya was stolen from the royal sanctum on Dhanteras eve. Darkness threatens the festival of Diwali. Your task is to investigate 15 locations, interview 8 suspects, solve escape chambers to gather the 7 Flame Fragments, and accuse the true culprit before the festival concludes.

🔍 2. Searching Districts & Gathering Relics

Visit the Investigation section and choose any unlocked district. Searching triggers unique events, yields coins, clues, and rare satchel items. Re-investigating districts may uncover deeper secrets.

🔒 3. Escape Chambers & Riddles

Crack combinations, play Rangoli memory pairs, solve logic puzzles, and test your wit with 200 bespoke festival riddles. Correct answers grant coins, XP, and critical Evidence Board items.

👑 4. The 5 Endings

Once all 7 Flame Fragments are unified, the Final Accusation Chamber unlocks. Accusing the correct culprit with sufficient evidence unlocks the Master Detective Ending or Festival Savior Ending. Reckless accusations lead to the Wrong Accusation Ending or Partial Success Ending.

📱 5. Progressive Web App (PWA) Offline Play

This application is a 100% standalone PWA. Install it on your home screen or desktop. It functions seamlessly without an active internet connection using integrated offline state caches and synthesized audio.
