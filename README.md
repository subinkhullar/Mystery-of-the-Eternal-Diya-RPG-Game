# Mystery-of-the-Eternal-Diya-RPG-Game
🪔 Mystery of the Eternal Diya puts you in the role of a royal detective on a mission to recover a sacred Diwali lamp stolen before the Festival. Explore magical locations, solve riddles, escape puzzle rooms, collect flame fragments, investigate suspects, unlock achievements, track stats, and restore light to the kingdom in this adventure. ✨🏆🔍📜
